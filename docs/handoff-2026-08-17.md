## The App Store rejection (submission `94b8ca7b-893d-4554-ac99-7079bec53619`)

Reviewed 2026-08-17 on iPhone 17 Pro Max, version 1.2.2 (16).

### 1. Guideline 2.5.4, Bluetooth background modes — FIXED (`ee4c619`, unpushed)

`UIBackgroundModes` declared `bluetooth-central` and `bluetooth-peripheral` with
no Core Bluetooth anywhere in the app (no `import CoreBluetooth`, no
`CBCentralManager`, no `CBPeripheral`). Apple was factually correct. They came in
with the glasses spike (`aeadecb`, Aug 9) on the assumption that anything
Bluetooth needed them; the glasses actually used Bluetooth *Classic* via
ExternalAccessory, which is a different mode entirely.

Both removed. Verified absent from the generated `Glutt/Info.plist`.

### 2 and 3. Guideline 3.1.2(c), subscriptions — DIAGNOSED, NOT FIXED

Two separate complaints, both about the same screen:

- the app does not show the **price** of the subscription (and price per unit)
- the **free trial** does not say how long it lasts or what is billed after

**Where the fault is.** The app's own Swift contains no subscription price
anywhere; I grepped it, every price hit is groceries or recipe costing. Title,
length, price and trial terms all live in the Superwall-hosted paywall, served at
runtime, which is not in this repo.

**Direct evidence.** Earlier in the session I rendered the live paywall on the
simulator and screenshotted it. It read: "Glutt PRO / Cook anything you actually
want / ✓ No commitment, cancel anytime / **Try 3 days free**". No price, no
subscription length, no auto-renew disclosure. That is exactly what Apple wrote
up, twice.

**What the fix needs to add** to the paywall:

- subscription title and length
- price, and price per unit if used. Prefer Superwall's dynamic bindings
  (`products.primary.price`) over static literals so other storefronts are
  correct; `docs/HARD-PAYWALL-FREE-TRIAL.md` records that they were left as
  static literals ($49.99 / $0.96 per week / $7.99)
- explicit trial terms, e.g. "7 days free, then $49.99/year. Auto-renews unless
  cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends."
- Terms of Use and Privacy Policy links in the paywall footer

**Also check in App Store Connect metadata:** Apple's message requires functional
Privacy Policy and Terms of Use links there, not only in the app. Settings does
have both (`glutt.org/privacy`, `glutt.org/terms`), but
`docs/REENABLE-PAYMENTS.md` still lists the ASC metadata item as unchecked.

## Running it on Malik's phone

Device: `C726E36B-DA09-56B8-85D1-2B19FEB8E129`, an iPhone 15 Plus named 😵‍💫.

Use **environment variables, not launch arguments**. `devicectl` splits a launch
argument into single letters, so any flag containing an "l" (`-realGates`,
`-relaxGates`) fails with "Missing value for '-l <path>'". Environment variables
pass through untouched and both latch in Debug, so they survive the user tapping
the app icon.

| Variable | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| `GLUTT_RELAX_GATES=1` | skips Superwall entirely, app runs open. **This is what he usually wants.** |
| `GLUTT_REAL_GATES=1` | Superwall on, real paywall, app locked without a subscription |

Two recurring device annoyances, both benign: the first install often fails with
"connection was invalidated", and simply retrying works; and a launch fails
outright if the phone is locked.

## Open threads, none urgent, all real

**The SwiftData crash is contained, not cured** (`8b88022`, on `main`). A recipe
holding a link to a deleted `RecipeCollection` crashed the app on launch before
any UI, for signed-in users only. I fixed the two read sites. Still broken
underneath: the inverse on `Recipe.collections` is not nullifying in every path,
and `MealPlan.collection` has **no inverse and no delete rule at all**. I never
reproduced the trigger from clean, so it is unproven whether it needs a sign out
and back in, or whether the ordinary delete-a-collection button does it. Worth
settling, because that decides the blast radius.

**Recipe images go blank on restore, and it is mostly a drip problem.** Nothing
expires image bytes; there is no staleness. `RecipeImageSync` uploads 5 and
downloads 10 per sweep, one sweep per foreground, so a reinstall returns photos
ten per app open and a photo deleted before its upload turn is gone for good.
Malik's constant reinstalling amplifies it; the same gap hits a real user getting
a new phone. Suggested fixes: loop the sweeps while a restore is outstanding,
prioritise upload by rot risk (Instagram and TikTok URLs are unrecoverable,
YouTube and Spoonacular are not), and distinguish "waiting on download" from "no
photo" in the UI.

**Glasses.** Removed in `f206d2f`; `docs/glasses-removal.md` is the full
inventory and the restore path (`git revert f206d2f && xcodegen generate`).
Malik's friend was going to own making the SDK and its Info.plist keys
conditional per build configuration; the removal makes that unnecessary for now,
but it is the right shape if glasses ever return.
