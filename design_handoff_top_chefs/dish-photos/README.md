# Drop the 15 chef dish photos here

Any format (jpg, png, heic) and any size over 1600px wide. I crop, resize and
wire them up. Name each file as listed, extension aside.

Good sources: Pexels, Unsplash, Freepik, your own camera.

**What makes a photo work here:** landscape, the dish filling the frame, shot
from overhead or at a 45 degree angle, no text, no watermark, no restaurant
branding. Cards crop to a wide band, so keep the dish centred and don't rely on
the edges.
