The cleanest way to group the app
To avoid feature soup, I’d group everything into 7 big product pillars:
Pillar
Main purpose
1. Import & Save
Bring recipes from anywhere into one clean library.
2. Search & Memory
Find recipes the way humans actually remember food.
3. Plan & Remind
Plan meals by day/time and get cooking/prep reminders.
4. Pantry & Grocery
Know what you have, what you need, and what to buy.
5. Cook & Adapt
Cook step-by-step and adapt recipes to your real kitchen.
6. Eat & Track
Log what you actually ate, calories/macros, leftovers, goals.
7. Discover & Share
Get recommendations, trending recipes, and optionally share meals.

The features I’d call the “killer” ones
These are the ones that feel most like a real company, not just another recipe app:
Killer Feature
Why it matters
Semantic recipe memory
Solves “I saved 300 recipes but can’t find the one I want.”
Video/social recipe import that actually works
Solves messy TikTok/Instagram recipe saving better than basic importers.
Pantry/fridge camera inventory
Removes the most annoying part of pantry apps: typing everything manually.
Optimize recipe for what I have
Turns the app into a real cooking assistant, not just a database.
Planned vs actual eaten tracking
Bridges cooking app and fitness app in a way most apps don’t.
Leftovers tracking
Very real daily-life cooking problem that most apps barely handle.
Cooking start/prep reminders
Makes meal planning actually actionable.
Taste profile learning
Makes recommendations improve over time instead of being generic.
